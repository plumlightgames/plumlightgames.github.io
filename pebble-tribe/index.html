Privacy Policy

Who we are

Pebble Tribe is published by Plumlight Games. Questions about your data: rgg3317@hotmail.com

What stays on your phone

Your progress, your settings and the name and picture you choose are kept on your device only. We never receive them. If your phone's own backup is on, it may keep a copy in your own account (for example Google).

Anonymous play data - only if you allow it

If you say yes, the game sends: a random number made on your device (it stands for you; it is not your name, your account or your advertising ID), which levels are started, won and lost and on which try, which ad offers are used, how long a visit lasts, your device model, system version and game version. The service that receives this also sees your internet address (IP), as every web service does. We use it only to see where the game is too hard and to improve it. We do not sell it and do not use it to find out who you are.

Who receives it

The data goes to GameAnalytics (gameanalytics.com), which keeps the statistics for us. Its servers may be outside your country. The legal basis is your consent. You can take it back at any time under Settings > Play data: that stops the sending and deletes what was still waiting on your device. The data is kept for as long as these statistics need it, or until you ask for it to be deleted.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a short video you choose to watch for a reward and, only between levels, a full-screen ad. To show ads, limit how often you see them and measure them, Google may use your device's advertising identifier, its internet address (IP), device information and how ads are viewed and tapped. Where the law requires it (EU/EEA, UK, Switzerland) you are asked for consent first and can change your choice under Settings > Privacy. You can reset or turn off the advertising identifier in your phone's settings. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Notifications

Reminders (lives full, lucky wheel ready) are set by your own phone. Nothing is sent to us. You can switch them off in Settings or in your phone's settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age your country sets for consent to data use, up to 16). We do not knowingly collect data from them. With play data switched off, nothing is collected.

Your rights

You may ask what is held about you, have it deleted, object to its use, and complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: the KVKK board; in the EU: your national authority). To have your play data deleted, write to rgg3317@hotmail.com and include your player number, shown below.
