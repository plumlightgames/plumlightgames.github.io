Privacy Policy – Torqly (last updated 20.11.2026)

Torqly is made by Plumlight Games. Contact: rgg3317@hotmail.com

1. What the game keeps

Your progress (scores, levels, coins, themes, settings) is saved only on your phone. The game has no account and no server of its own; it does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, photos or location.

2. Notifications

If you allow it, the phone itself shows reminders (for example, when the daily pile is ready). They are scheduled on the phone; nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings.

3. Advertising

This version of the game shows no ads and contains no advertising code. If ads are added in a later update, this section will first be updated to say which ad network is used and what it collects.

4. Purchases

There is nothing to buy with real money. Every theme and booster is bought with coins earned in the game.

5. Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over.

6. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes all of its data from your phone. For questions or requests write to rgg3317@hotmail.com.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here and in the game.

Oyunda gerçek parayla satın alınan hiçbir şey yoktur. Her tema ve güçlendirici oyunda kazanılan altınla alınır.

5. Çocuklar

Oyun 13 yaş ve üzeri oyuncular içindir.

6. Verilerin silinmesi

Oyunu kaldırmak, telefonundaki bütün verilerini siler. Soruların ya da istekler için rgg3317@hotmail.com adresine yazabilirsin.
